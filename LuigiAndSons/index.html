The Heritage

Nonna's recipe.
No apologies.

In 1951, Nonna Rosa packed two things when she left Naples: her rosary and a handwritten recipe card tucked inside her apron. The dough — a 72-hour cold-fermented blend of Caputo 00 flour, water, sea salt, and a whisper of wild yeast — was her life's work.

Her son Luigi carried that card to Brooklyn in 1971, rolled up his sleeves, and opened a 12-seat counter on Mulberry Street. No frills, no shortcuts. Just San Marzano tomatoes, hand-pulled mozzarella, and the kind of crust that crackles at the edge and stays pillowy at the center.

Today his sons run the ovens. The recipe card is still on the wall — behind glass now, but unchanged.
